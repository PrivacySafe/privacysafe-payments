Secure payments, working for you.

John Kiriakou’s Secret Agent Club, a signup flow with direct payments. A listener pays with Solana in USDC and shares the episode in PrivacySafe Chat. Then a second listener pays in AUDD via x402.

What happens in the walkthrough videos?
  1. Introduction: Community. John Kiriakou’s Secret Agent Club is an exclusive, members-only community featuring episodes that listeners can unlock by paying the creator directly.
  2. Part One: Solana Pay. A listener signs in using their PrivacySafe address and visits the Secret Agent Club. They select “Join for 1.00 USDC”, with the option to switch between USDC and AUDD, and complete the payment using their cryptocurrency wallet. PrivacySafe verifies that the transaction has been finalized on the Solana blockchain before confirming “You’re in” and unlocking the episode. The listener can then share the episode through PrivacySafe Chat, save it to PrivacySafe Storage, and copy a link to the stored content.
  3. Part Two: x402 payments in the app. A second listener selects AUDD as their payment currency. PrivacySafe automatically detects compatible cryptocurrency wallets, displaying “Pay with Phantom (detected)”. Pressing and holding the button reveals Phantom and MetaMask as available options. The listener pays 1.40 AUDD using Phantom, and PrivacySafe unlocks the episode once the transaction is finalized on Solana.

Exclusive PrivacySafe Communities.

This is a live proof of concept: pay via the app or AI agents. Use Solana and x402, in USDC or AUDD, and unlock a private community. This extends end-to-end encrypted apps that already ship. Get them at privacysafe.app (opens in a new tab).
